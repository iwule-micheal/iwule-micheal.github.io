About

I'm Iwule Micheal Osinakachukwu, a junior copywriter based in Lagos, Nigeria. I like copywriting because it's persuasion done well: helping people discover something useful and giving them a clear reason to act. I've written essay, social media content, articles and marketing copy, and I've been building my copywriting skills with practice projects in packaging, social, email, product pages and landing pages. The projects here are speculative, written for fictional or uncommissioned briefs. I'm looking for part-time or entry-level copywriting work where I can learn and write for a brand I care about.

What I write

Packaging copy, social posts, promotional emails, e-commerce product descriptions and landing pages. I also edit and proofread.

Tools

Microsoft Word, Grammarly, Canva, Meta Business Suite, Slack, Notion and HTML basics. Basic awareness of Google Analytics and Search Console.
