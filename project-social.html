Kettle & Crumb: Weekday Warm-Up

A social campaign for an invented neighbourhood bakery.

Overview

A practice campaign for a made-up bakery that wants more people through the door on weekday mornings, not just at weekends.

Objective

Get locals to try a weekday-morning stop between home and work, and to follow the account.

Target audience

People aged 25 to 40 who live or work nearby, walk or commute past the shop, and treat breakfast as an afterthought.

Brand voice

Friendly, dry and brief. Small jokes, real details (times, flavours), no hype words like "delicious" or "artisan".

Copy

Instagram captions

1. The 7:40 bus will not wait for you. The croissant will.
We open at 7, Tuesday to Thursday. Come in, grab one, make the bus. Probably.

2. Cardamom bun. Flat white. Ten minutes.
That's the whole plan. Weekday mornings from 7.
Tag the person who needs a ten-minute break.

3. The ovens go on at 4:30 so your Wednesday doesn't have to start with sad toast.
Fresh bread is on the shelves when we open at 7.

Short posts

1. Wednesday needs a cardamom bun.

2. Open at 7. Still warm at 7:05.

3. Your desk lunch could be a sandwich on bread we baked this morning. Just saying.

Hooks

  • The 7:40 bus will not wait for you.
  • Wednesday needs a cardamom bun.
  • Five minutes. One bun. A better Tuesday.

Call-to-action options

  • Come in before work.
  • Tag your coffee buddy.
  • Save this for Thursday.
  • Follow for the daily bake.

Strategy

The first line does the work. In a feed, people see only the opening line or two before deciding to read on, so each caption starts with its best hook and saves the details for after.

One idea per post. Each caption sells one thing: the croissant, the bun, the fresh bread. Social readers skim, so a second idea just gets lost.

Specific beats vague. "7:40 bus", "4:30 ovens" and "cardamom bun" are details a reader can picture. "Delicious pastries" is not.

Small asks. The CTAs are tiny actions (tag, save, follow) because most people scrolling aren't ready to make a big decision. The "come in" version is used sparingly.

Voice. Short sentences and one dry joke per post. The humour is mild so the brand stays friendly.

Skills demonstrated

Writing hooks, adapting copy to a platform, brand voice, concise writing, calls to action.
