Greenhand: Repotting Weekend

A promotional email for an invented houseplant shop.

Overview

A practice email for a made-up plant shop's autumn sale: 15% off pots and potting soil until Sunday 18 October.

Objective

Get past customers to click through and buy pots and soil, using a reason that is true and useful to them.

Target audience

Customers who bought a plant in the last six months. They already own a plant and care about it, so the email speaks to that plant.

Brand voice

Helpful and calm, like a friend who knows plants. No guilt, no shouting, no fake scarcity.

Copy

Subject lines

  • [First name], your [plant name] might need a bigger pot
  • Repotting weekend: 15% off pots and soil
  • Roots coming out the bottom? Read this

Preview text

Autumn is a good time to repot. Pots and soil are 15% off until Sunday 18 October.

Full email

Hi [First name],

If your [plant name] has roots poking out of the drainage hole, or dries out a day after you water it, it's probably asking for more room.

Autumn is a good time to repot. Growth slows down, so most houseplants cope with the move more easily.

This weekend, pots and potting soil are 15% off. All you need is:

  • A pot 2 to 5 cm wider than the current one
  • A bag of potting soil
  • About twenty minutes

Shop pots and soil (button)

The offer ends Sunday 18 October at 11:59pm. No code needed, the discount is applied at checkout.

Not sure it's time? Reply with a photo of your plant and we'll tell you.

The Greenhand team

P.S. Our two-minute repotting guide is here: greenhand.example/repotting

Shorter version

Hi [First name],

Roots coming out of the drainage hole? Autumn is a good time to repot. Pots and potting soil are 15% off until Sunday 18 October.

Shop pots and soil (button)

Strategy

Subject lines. Three different jobs: one personal (uses the customer's plant), one clear (says what the offer is), one curious (makes you wonder what to read). In a real send, I'd test them against each other.

Personalisation. The first name and plant name show this was written for the reader. A fallback ("Hi there") should be set in case a name is missing.

Benefits before the discount. The email opens with the reader's problem and the reason to act now, then mentions the price. A discount alone gives no reason to care.

Honest urgency. The deadline is a real date and time. I avoided "only a few left" and countdown pressure, because nothing in the brief supports them.

Readability. Short paragraphs, one list, one button. The CTA names what the reader gets ("Shop pots and soil"), not "Click here".

Skills demonstrated

Subject-line writing, personalisation, persuasion without pressure, clear calls to action, writing for readability, cutting a long version down.
