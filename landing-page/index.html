  1. Add your bills

    Enter the name and due date. Repeat monthly if it comes back.

    Decide how early you want the nudge.

Questions

Does Dueday pay my bills for me?

No. It reminds you and keeps track. You still pay each bill the way you normally do.

Do I need to connect my bank account?

No. You add the bill name and due date yourself.

What does it cost?

You can start free. Free for up to five bills. Dueday Plus is £3 a month for unlimited bills.

What if I miss a reminder?

You can set more than one, for example a week before and a day before.
